| `symmetric-binary-btree` (1972); `red-black-tree` (1978) | [2–3–4 tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2%E2%80%933%E2%80%934_tree): a 2–3–4 tree is an order-four B-tree related to red–black trees; [Red–black tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red%E2%80%93black_tree), History: Bayer called his 1972 order-four B-tree a symmetric binary B-tree; Guibas and Sedgewick derived red–black trees in their 1978 paper. | A supported two-step chain from existing B-tree indexing through the symmetric form to the later binary representation. | `b-plus-tree` (1973) | [B+ tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%2B_tree), History: a 1973 IBM publication describes use in VSAM; the concept is a leaf-record variant of Bayer and McCreight's 1970 B-tree. | Distinct storage-oriented B-tree variant. The audit flags this alternate path, retained for that separately supported storage role. | `linear-hashing` (1980) | [Linear hashing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_hashing): Litwin's dynamic hash table grows by splitting one bucket at a time and avoids periodic full file reorganization. | Direct extension of the existing hash-table capability; no claim that database products adopted it at its inception. |

| `entity-relationship-model` (1976) | [Entity–relationship model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entity%E2%80%93relationship_model): Chen developed and published the model in 1976; entities, attributes, and relationships can be mapped into relational tables and keys. | Retained as a distinct conceptual modeling technique. | `data-warehouse` (1988) | [Data warehouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_warehouse), History: Devlin and Murphy's 1988 article introduced the business data warehouse architecture for flow from operational systems to decision support. | Year is the dated architecture publication, not a claimed first warehouse. | `timsort` (2002) | [Timsort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timsort): Peters implemented it for Python; the stable algorithm derives from merge sort and insertion sort, detects existing runs, and merges them. | Existing merge sort is retained as a direct parent.
