## Date/scope spot-checks

- The crow's-foot node is scoped to the notation's beginnings in Gordon Everest's 1976 article, not its later use in Barker notation, SSADM, or CASE tools. The checked ER-model page explicitly distinguishes this line/cardinality notation from Chen's own diagrams and says some crow's-foot diagram conventions owed more to earlier Bachman diagrams. The Bachman node is independently scoped to the cited 1969 *Data structure diagrams* publication; this is the relationship used by the authored edge.
- Powersort is scoped to the 2018 proposal in Munro and Wild's *Nearly-Optimal Mergesorts*. The checked Powersort page describes the original algorithm as a drop-in replacement for Timsort's merge policy, while separately dating *multiway* Powersort to 2023. The node does not claim the 2018 authors proposed that later variant.
